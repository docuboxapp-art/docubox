## Requested outcome

Please identify the proximate failure and underlying cause of simultaneous 5xx responses from Supabase Auth and PostgREST; provide a UTC event timeline, request-level correlation, resource/pool evidence, the reason for the observed restart transition, and a specific mitigation with validation criteria. ## Customer impact and application boundary

Docubox users saw slow or failed sign-in, session verification errors, and slow document navigation. The Supabase gateway recorded errors on `/auth/v1/token`, Auth JWKS and `/rest/v1/rpc/enforce_docubox_session_policy`; earlier Vercel Runtime Errors included `PGRST002` on document routes. The currently deployed application had retries and repeated verification calls that could amplify latency; local corrections are **not deployed**. Those application behaviors do not explain why Supabase returned the recorded 5xx. Protected Docubox operations remain subject to authorization checks; we have not disabled them.
